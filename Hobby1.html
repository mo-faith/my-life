Football ⚽

Watching and playing football has been a massive part of my life. Watching football started back in 2016 when I was 9. Liverpool FC was the first club I supported and the last one I will. One of the best things to ever happen to me was looking up to Cristiano Ronaldo. Him as a player and human being really changed me as a human. The hardwork and determination as a player. The humility and kindness he possess as a human inspired me to be who I am. When I was a kid I wanted to become a footballer, but I did not become it, but I continue to play it because I enjoy it.
